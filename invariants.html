The Periodic Table of Agent Invariants

A working census of the invariants that let an artificial system establish its operational state — and the authority each established state can honestly justify. Organized by what this tree can say about each one, which for most of them is: not much yet.

Compiled by Ampersand Box Design opensentience.org graphonomous.com
v0.8 · What changed The table used to be grouped by which spec folder owned each cell — Group I is Graphonomous, Group IV is Delegatic — across 10 protocol groups. That is an org chart wearing a periodic table's clothes, and it has a specific cost: an empty slot in it means nobody has written that spec yet, which is an editorial fact and predicts nothing. Mendeleev's gaps were predictions because his groups shared a structural property. These shared a maintainer.

So v0.8 groups the cells by what this tree can say about them. Four registers, each with a mechanical rule re-derived on every build: something here decides the cell, or code exists and no proposition has been settled, or it has a name and nothing else, or it is a proposal. Inside the decided register the axis is the semantic kind — the 6 kind tokens mosaic/occupancy.json derived by enumerating what the table actually uses. That is the axis cell 27a asked for in round 3, when it argued the table needs orthogonal axes and that “Flow: yes or no” is the wrong question to force.

What the regrouping made visible, and this is the point of it. 13 of 46 cells have an evidence anchor. 22 have a name, a protocol home, a stated authority, and nothing that decides them. The ledger beside this page holds 182 claims and only 7 cells receive any of them — 54 bound claims in all, of which 14 are REFUTED. The old layout had no way to show that: there was no refuted shading, so nine rounds spent mostly killing propositions rendered as an unbroken accumulation of achievement. κ does not carry the federation obstruction it was published as carrying; aggregation fidelity is false in its universal form; escalation monotonicity is false in its universal form. Each of those is a result, and the page reported none of them.

Two things this page now declines to do. It no longer calls a property test a proof — a bounded randomized search over a generator's range corroborates a universal and cannot discharge it, which is what LED-C9 cost when it shipped PROVED on two thousand trials and was refuted by an operation the generator never reached. And it no longer lets adjacency do the work of evidence: the economic addendum, which has no ledger record, no witness and no obligation between its cells, is annexed rather than distributed through the measured table with a 6 px dot.

What is still authored, stated so it can be attacked. 6 authored kinds were assigned during the regroup, each read off the cell's own source line and each carrying the line it was read off — open a cell to see it. 1 decided cell is deliberately left unassigned, because “history cannot be rewritten” reads as Conservation, as Structural and as Authority, and no criterion in this tree picks one. Forcing it would be the TAX-COMPOUND error: quantifying over a taxonomy nobody can produce. And the shading inside a register remains editorial — the v0.6 note disclosed that no criterion is stated for what earns proved over named, and that disclosure still stands for the shading. What v0.8 adds is that the axis now carrying the page's structure has a rule you can run.

The axis

Cells are grouped by what the tree can say about them, not by which spec folder owns them. The rule for each band is mechanical and re‑derived on every build.

A Decided 13 cells A proposition about this cell has been settled, and something in the tree reproduces it. cell.tier is set, OR at least one CLAIM_LEDGER.json claim binds `cell:<num>` at a settled status (PROVED · CONDITIONAL · REFUTED · KNOWN · MEASURED).
B Built, not decided 4 cells An implementation exists. No proposition about it has been settled here. not decided, AND cell.status is "shipped".
C Named 22 cells A name, a protocol home, and a stated authority consequence. Nothing decides it. not decided, not built, not economic.
D Proposed 7 cells A design proposal. No ledger record, no witness, no obligation. cell.economic is true.

Evidence anchor

Machine-checked Complete finite enumeration, or a deductive proof with an executable check. Property-tested Randomized search over a generator's range. Corroboration, not discharge — what the generator cannot reach is where the counterexample lives. Implementation-verified A test suite passes against one implementation. Scoped to that implementation.

Shading

proved A result is claimed and an evidence anchor exists. Read the tier: the anchors are not of one strength. conditional Proved under an antecedent that is NOT discharged. The antecedent is on the cell, and a conditional cell that does not name it is refused by the build. shipped Running code. Says nothing about whether a proposition has been settled. named The invariant has a name, a home and a stated authority. Nothing decides it. sketched A shape has been described. Neither named precisely nor decided. missing The slot is identified and empty.
