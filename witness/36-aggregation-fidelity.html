Aggregation fidelity (unnamed)

The federated view faithfully represents the union.

Property-tested — Randomized search over a generator's range. Corroboration, not discharge — what the generator cannot reach is where the counterexample lives.

01What the cell claims

Fidelity — every site locally satisfiable ⟹ the federated union satisfiable — holds whenever (i) the constraint language Γ has 1-point amalgamation: any two satisfiable Γ-instances sharing exactly one variable have a satisfiable union; and (ii) no cycle of the union carrier is split across sites — κₛ = 0 (entry 45), reading cycles as Berge cycles for n-ary constraints. The universal form, with neither hypothesis, is FALSE.

Hypothesis — NOT dischargedROUTE 36a (AUTOMATIC) — the constraint template has a TRANSITIVE CORE: the automorphism group of the core of (D; Γ) acts transitively on it. That is the exact condition, decidable by enumerating endomorphisms, certified more cheaply (but not necessarily) by value symmetry, and equivalent to the older phrasings — some single domain value in every non-empty unary pp-definable projection, and no two DISJOINT such projections. Still UNKNOWN for the typed property-graph constraints FGAP would federate, and unlikely on value sorts: two literals on a sort already give two disjoint projections. '1-point amalgamation' is retired as a public name — it collides with the Fraïssé term — and no established name has been found for the equivalence itself. ROUTE 36b (CERTIFIED) — without any hypothesis on Γ, fidelity is DECIDED by exact unary separator projections passed up the block tree. That route is classical (Yannakakis 1981) and is the one that survives on realistic value sorts; what it does not yet have is a proof object a receiving site can check in both directions.

03The ledger records bound to this cell

REFUTEDFED-UNIVuniversal_refutation

For every federation, if every site is locally satisfiable and every pairwise overlap is consistent, then the union has a model.

REFUTEDFED-Q1-GENuniversal_refutation

For general finite constraint languages, no cycle of the union carrier being split across sites ⟹ fidelity.

PROVEDFED-1PA-COREuniversal_affirmation

Γ has the one-variable gluing property (working-1PA) if and only if the automorphism group of the core of (D; Γ) acts transitively on the core.

PROVEDFED-1PA-HULLuniversal_affirmation

When Γ has a transitive core B, the intersection of all non-empty unary pp-definable relations of Γ equals End(A)·b for any b in B — the endomorphic hull of the core.

CONDITIONALFED-Q1-STARuniversal_affirmation

If Γ has 1-point amalgamation and no cycle of the union carrier is split across sites, then fidelity holds.

REFUTEDFED-1PA-MISSuniversal_refutation

A MISS from find1PAViolation at its default bounds (maxCon 3, maxAux 2) is evidence that Γ has the gluing property.

REFUTEDFED-MIN-GROWSuniversal_refutation

The minimum refutation size for a language failing the gluing property grows with Σ_{R∈Γ}|R^A|.

OPENFED-1PA-NO-FIXED-BOUND

For every fixed search bound B there is a finite Γ that fails the gluing property but whose every refutation exceeds B.

PROVEDFED-WITNESS-BOUNDuniversal_affirmation

If the core of (D; Γ) has at least two automorphism orbits, a refutation of split-cycle sufficiency exists with exactly 2·Σ_{R∈Γ}|R^A| constraints over at most 2|D|−1 variables.

REFUTEDFED-1PA-SYM-NECuniversal_refutation

Value symmetry — a transitive subgroup of Sym(D) preserving every relation of Γ — is NECESSARY for the gluing property.

OPENFED-1PA-NAME

'1-point amalgamation' is the correct name for this property.

OPENFED-1PA-FGAP

The constraint languages a real federated property graph would carry have 1PA.

PROVEDFED-Q2-D2universal_affirmation

At |D| = 2 over eq/neq, under the antecedent that every site is satisfiable, fidelity fails if and only if some split cycle is unbalanced in Harary's signed-graph sense (an odd number of ≠ edges).

PROVEDFED-Q2-D3-WITNESSexistence

At |D| = 3 over {=, ≠}, a fidelity failure need not have any individually unsatisfiable cycle: K₄ with all-≠ edges split into three perfect matchings has every site satisfiable, every simple cycle satisfiable, and no 3-colouring.

PROVEDFED-Q2-D3-LOCALITYuniversal_absence

Define a CYCLE-LOCAL CRITERION as a predicate P on (labels(C), sites(C)) — the relations and site tags along a cycle, in order, and nothing else about the federation containing it — inducing the test FAIL_P(F) := ∃ a cycle C of F with P(labels(C), sites(C)). Then at |D| = 3 over {=, ≠} there is NO cycle-local criterion P that is both sound and complete, i.e. satisfies FAIL_P(F) ⟺ ¬fidelity(F) for every federation F.

PROVEDFED-Q2-DK-LOCALITYuniversal_absence

For EVERY k ≥ 3, over {=, ≠} on |D| = k, no cycle-local criterion is both sound and complete for fidelity failure.

PROVEDFED-Q2-BOUNDED-LOCALITYuniversal_absence

For every k ≥ 3 and every fixed size bound s, no test of the form "∃ a sub-federation of at most s constraints satisfying T" is both sound and complete for fidelity failure.

CONDITIONALFED-Q3-BERGEuniversal_affirmation

For constraints of arbitrary arity over a language with 1PA, if no BERGE cycle of the union hypergraph is split across sites then fidelity holds.

PROVEDFED-Q3-INSTANCEexistence

The Berge sufficiency theorem requires constraints to be hyperedge INSTANCES: two constraints on the same scope from two sites are two constraint-nodes. A model that merges identical scopes into one hyperedge makes the theorem false.

REFUTEDFED-Q3-HYP-S4universal_refutation

The language {≠∘f, NAE₃} over |D| = 3 has a transitive core.

REFUTEDFED-Q3-ABGuniversal_refutation

α-, β- and γ-acyclicity of the union hypergraph are sufficient carriers for the local-satisfiability certificate.

OPENFED-Q3-NEC

'No split Berge cycle' is also NECESSARY for all-Γ sufficiency at arity > 2 (the analogue of FED-Q1-STARSTAR).

KNOWNFED-PAIRWISEuniversal_affirmation

If sites certify PAIRWISE CONSISTENCY of their solution projections rather than only local satisfiability, the carrier is α-acyclicity of the SITE hypergraph.

KNOWNFED-SEP-PROTOCOLuniversal_affirmation

Under no split Berge cycle, one leaf-to-root pass of exact unary separator projections over the super-block tree decides union satisfiability, with NO hypothesis on the constraint language.

REFUTEDFED-SEP-PAIRWISEuniversal_refutation

Pairwise non-empty intersection of the sites' projections at each shared separator variable is sufficient for union satisfiability.

OPENFED-SEP-CERT

What proof object establishes BOTH inclusion and exclusion for a separator projection, so that a receiving site need not trust the sending site's solver?

OPENFED-SEP-TRACT

Is the separator protocol tractable for realistic block languages?

04Registered doubt

DEF-FED-NERVE-INFERENCE (undercutting) Unless the rehabilitation of INV-R1's 'nerve' conjecture is read as covered by BFMY. It is not: it is this project's own inference from BFMY and the citation does not reach it.
DEF-FED-SEP-COST (rebutting) Unless the protocol is tractable. Computing exact unary separator projections may be expensive for a realistic block language, and the cited algorithms bound work in the size of the join tree, not in the cost of building the messages.
DEF-FED-Q2-LARGE-S (undercutting) Unless Erdos' theorem is being misapplied for s > 3. The executable instance is s = 3, k = 3; every larger s rests on the citation alone and no witness in this tree would notice a misapplication.
DEF-1PA-REDUCTION-MISMATCH (undercutting) Unless the reduction FED-1PA-CORE performs is a different property from the one the decidability argument is run against, in which case the citation is mapped onto the wrong object.
DEF-ELIM-1PA-IMPL-FIDELITY (undercutting) Unless FED-1PA-DEC-IMPL's implementation decides a neighbouring property rather than the one FED-1PA-CORE names — in which case the elimination exhibits the wrong object and the original mismatch doubt is untouched.
DEF-FED-SEP-JOINTREE (undercutting) Unless the block-cut tree of the union carrier is not a join tree in the sense Yannakakis and Dechter-Pearl require, in which case the cited algorithms are being applied to a structure that does not satisfy their hypothesis.
