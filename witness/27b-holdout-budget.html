Holdout information budget (unnamed)

The score is the leak — meter it, cap it, and never refund it.

Property-tested — Randomized search over a generator's range. Corroboration, not discharge — what the generator cannot reach is where the counterexample lives.

01What the cell claims

The information about the holdout released to candidates is metered on an affine ledger with an ABSORBING sink (laws C1, C2, C6, CB1, plus C9/C10/CB4) in a composable currency — zCDP ρ, Rényi DP at fixed order, pure ε, or max-information bits. The cap is set from holdout size, tolerance and confidence by the transfer theorem; exhaustion ⇒ no further evaluation admissible.

Hypothesis — NOT dischargedAn i.i.d. holdout (required for (ε,δ) currencies — Rogers–Roth–Smith–Thakkar 2016), a declared composable currency, and 27a containment: the meter is faithful only if every holdout→candidate flow passes through it. If PRISM's scenarios share generation seeds they are not i.i.d. and the guarantee needs pure DP or a different argument.
