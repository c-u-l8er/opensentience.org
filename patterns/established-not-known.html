Established, Not Known

Establish, never know or detect: an observation can be stale, forged or partial, and establishing is what survives that.

Intent

Operational state is established, not known. To establish is to take an observation through freshness, provenance and completeness before it becomes state a locus acts on. A stale observation is still established — it was once true and is marked old. An unestablished one was never an observation at all. 'Position' is deprecated as the technical term; say established operational state.

Technical register

AGENCY.md §4 vocabulary. STALE ≠ UNESTABLISHED (Super bot spec). This is vocabulary, so its counterexample is a sentence and its nearest witness is the ontology lint; the record is a definition and carries no rung.

Problem

'The agent detected that the door is locked' reads as fact and is a reading of a reading. Copy written with know and detect grants observations a certainty they never earned, and systems built on that copy act on forgeries with the same confidence as on truths.

Solution

Vocabulary: establish, established operational state, stale, unestablished. Retire know, detect, position. Run the lint.

Real-world analogy

A ship's log entry versus a rumour on deck. The log entry can be old; the rumour was never an observation.

Where the vocabulary is set (AGENCY.md §4) AGENCY.md:172

### State does not grant authority — the ambient-authority trap

Forces

Establish is a longer word and a longer process, and it produces a state that can go stale — which readers experience as the system being unsure. Know is shorter and never unsure, which is the problem.

Applicability

Every technical sentence about what an agent has observed.

Transformations

Allowed
  • marking an established state stale
  • refusing an observation with no provenance
Forbidden
  • know / detect in technical copy
  • treating stale as unestablished, or the reverse

Consequences

The system's confidence becomes a property of its evidence rather than its prose.

Witness

No witness. A definition carries no evidence rung (AGENCY.md §6).

Counterexample

No counterexample shipped (required only when WITNESSED).

What to take away

  1. from the animationThe naive reader knew the stale reading and the forgery alike; the establishing reader marked one stale and refused the other.
  2. from the syntaxTwo words retired, one adopted; the difference is whether the sentence can be wrong.
  3. from the literatureByzantine observation; Gettier's problem, if you like — a true belief with the wrong provenance.
  4. from the witnessA definition; no rung; the lint on the neighbouring pattern is the falsifier.

Prior art

Byzantine observation: an observation can be stale, forged or partial; 'establish' names what survives that. STALE ≠ UNESTABLISHED (Super bot spec).
